Bacha Hua Khana design system

The visual language of an Indian commercial kitchen. Every food report is a kitchen order ticket; colours come from banana leaf, haldi, mirchi and stainless steel. Warm, practical, trustworthy. Rules and reasons are in DESIGN.md; pages use /assets/.

Colour

Ratios are WCAG contrast against the colour named. AA needs 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large text, icons and control borders.

  • Leaf --leaf #1E5631

    Primary actions, on the way, success. White text on it 8.6:1; on rice 8.3:1.

  • Leaf soft --leaf-soft #E3EFE4

    Success backgrounds. Leaf text on it 7.3:1.

  • Haldi --haldi #F2A900

    New report, live, recording. Background only: ink on it 7.9:1, white only 2.0:1.

  • Haldi soft --haldi-soft #FFF4D6

    Attention backgrounds. Ink on it 14.4:1.

  • Mirchi --mirchi #C62E1F

    Deadlines, declines, errors, "nahi". On white 5.5:1; white on it 5.5:1.

  • Mirchi soft --mirchi-soft #FBE6E3

    Error backgrounds. Mirchi text on it 4.6:1.

  • Steel --steel #D5DADF

    Dividers, decoration, inactive fills. Only 1.4:1 on white, so never a control's only border.

  • Steel dark --steel-dark #5F6A74

    Secondary text and control borders. On rice 5.3:1; on steel 3.9:1 (large text only).

  • Rice --rice #FAFAF7

    Page background.

  • Paper --paper #FFFFFF

    The KOT slip and inputs.

  • Ink --ink #17261C

    Body text. On rice 15.1:1.

Type

Baloo 2 for display, numbers and buttons (Baloo Chettan 2 for Malayalam, Baloo Tamma 2 for Kannada). Hind for body text, with Noto Sans Malayalam and Kannada behind it. All self-hosted.

Buttons

14px corners, at least 56px tall; 88px on kitchen and volunteer screens. The label says exactly what happens.

Status

Colour, icon and word together, never colour alone.

KOT slip

Every food report is a kitchen order ticket. It fills in line by line as the app understands the kitchen, then travels through the app. A value not heard yet is a blank dashed line. The pickup countdown is ink while there is time and turns mirchi at 30 minutes, so red always means hurry. The only element with a shadow.

Heard so far: the dish.

Reading from a metre away

Kitchen and volunteer screens put class="reading-far" on the body: body text steps up to 20px and small text to 16px. NGO and dashboard screens keep the base sizes.

    Rules to keep

    Copy

    • Sentence case. No all-caps, no labels above headings, no arrows on buttons.
    • Primary language big, English small underneath.

    Motion

    • Only after an action or a live event: recording pulse, a line writing in, a ticket moving.
    • Nothing animates on load except the first ticket. Reduced motion turns it all off.

    Access

    • AA contrast, 3px leaf focus ring, 56px targets (88px on kitchen and volunteer).
    • Works at 360px with no sideways scroll.
